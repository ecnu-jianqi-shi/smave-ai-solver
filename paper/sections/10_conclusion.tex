\label{sec:conclusion}

and terminal cost, including an NP-complete adjacent-interaction model. V6 reaches
$\SuiteRouteRegretPrecise\times$ oracle regret and
$\SuiteRouteFixedImprovementPercent\%$ below fixed with zero correctness, order, or
DP mismatch; frozen v5 switches no request and remains
$\SuiteRouteVFiveReplayVsFixed\times$ fixed. Together with retained regressions, this
supports workload-qualified verified cascade composition---not solver-selection
novelty, general robustness, or universal speedup.
