\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

V6 nearly matches oracle abstention but improves fixed by only
$\SuiteRouteFixedImprovementPercent\%$; frozen v5 switches no request and remains
$\SuiteRouteVFiveReplayVsFixed\times$ fixed. Brier/ECE
$\SuiteRouteBrier$/$\SuiteRouteEce$ and p95 cost error
$\SuiteRouteCostPNinetyFiveError$ preclude calibrated-model claims. Solver selection
and components are prior art; the contribution is gate-controlled verified
complete-path composition.

External validity is narrow: all timing uses one Apple M4, and the PDEBench-derived,
resource-limited COPS, one-HINTS, and three-matrix/24-request v5/v6 studies establish
neither architecture nor population performance; opposite v5/v6 outcomes show mixed
validity. Multi-host validation was attempted on a second ARM64 host (Nvidia DGX Station)
but could not complete due to 40--60$\times$ performance penalty on large matrices; SMAVE
build and execution succeeded, but platform suitability for timing benchmarks varies.
A post-hoc zero-execution audit finds \FrozenAttritionSupportedPairs{}
development-supported pairs, all absent at unguarded top-3 selection. Five different
unsupported candidates appear across \FrozenAttritionUnguardedCandidateRequests{} v6
requests, but the control-aware gate removes all; final candidates and timings are zero.
Development--held-out overlap is zero (v5/v6 held-out sets
\FrozenInteractionVFiveHeldoutPairs{}/\FrozenInteractionVSixHeldoutPairs{}, Jaccard
\FrozenInteractionHeldoutJaccard{}), so no multiplier or interaction benefit is inferred.
One PDEBench-derived case exceeds
$100\times$; eight SuiteSparse cases lack common success; device, nonlinear, topology,
and order effects remain. Broader prefrozen cohorts, architectures, and independent
reruns are required.

Residual gates cannot detect wrong callbacks, faulty arithmetic, or unavailable truth;
small residual may mask state error in ill-conditioned systems~\cite{jiang2026ens}.
Claims are only to declared equation/constraint tolerances. The interaction optimizer
models adjacent costs, not longer-memory costs or history-dependent acceptance; general
high-index reduction is excluded.
